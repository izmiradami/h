Privacy Policy

Kur'an Nuru – Woodstone Studio

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Woodstone Studio ("we", "our", or "us") built the Kur'an Nuru app as a free app. This page informs users about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our service:

  • Location data: Used only to calculate accurate prayer times and list nearby mosques based on your current location. Location data is processed on-device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Usage data: Anonymous analytics data (via Firebase Analytics) to understand how users interact with the app.
  • Push notification tokens: Used to send prayer time reminders and daily notifications.

2. User-Generated Content

The app includes a "Kur'an Chat" feature that allows users to ask questions about Islamic topics. Messages sent through this feature are processed by an AI service to generate responses. We do not store, share, or sell the content of these conversations. Chat messages are used solely to generate the requested response in real time.

Users are responsible for the content they submit. We reserve the right to disable access to this feature if it is used in a manner that violates our terms or applicable laws.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate prayer times and find nearby mosques (location)
  • To send prayer time reminders (push notifications)
  • To improve app performance and user experience (analytics)

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Firebase (Google): Analytics, push notifications, and authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Claude API: Powers the Kur'an Chat AI feature. Messages are processed per Anthropic's privacy policy.

5. Data Retention

We do not retain personal data on our servers. Location data is processed entirely on your device. Anonymous analytics data is retained by Firebase per Google's data retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any data associated with your device by contacting us at the email below. You can also disable location permissions and push notifications at any time via your device settings.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.
